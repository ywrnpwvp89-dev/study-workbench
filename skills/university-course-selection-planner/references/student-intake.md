# Student intake schema

Collect only information needed to identify applicable academic rules and preferences.

## Required or usually useful

- institution, college, campus, major, degree level, grade, class/program, academic year, and term;
- curriculum-plan version, track/stream, placement results, preassigned courses, completed prerequisites, and exemptions;
- preferred credit range, unavailable time periods, accessibility or commuting needs, and academic goals;
- desired number of plans and positioning.

## Privacy boundary

Never ask for passwords, one-time codes, MFA answers, student numbers, identity documents, payment details, or account recovery information. The student must complete login and final enrollment actions themselves. Redact personal identifiers from screenshots and uploaded examples.

## Missing answers

Use `默认假设` for harmless preferences only. Treat missing placement, prerequisite, eligibility, or curriculum-version information as `待确认`; do not silently infer it.
