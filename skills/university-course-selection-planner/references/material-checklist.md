# Tailored material checklist

Use the student's institution and program information to create a specific upload list. Do not prescribe an unverified URL.

| Material | Why it matters | Useful fields or evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Current curriculum plan | Total and module credit requirements | version, applicable cohort, module, course, credits, term |
| Major/college enrollment guide | Special rules and pitfalls | required courses, restrictions, caps, prerequisites |
| Current term catalog/timetable | Sections and meeting events | code, section, name, credits, teacher, time, weeks, room |
| Official selectable-section view | Eligibility and current availability | student population, capacity/status, prerequisites |
| Academic calendar/exam schedule | Exam and teaching-period conflicts | teaching weeks, exam date/time, holidays |
| Placement/exemption results | Placement-dependent choices | language/math level, exemptions, assigned group |
| Cross-campus or category rules | Feasibility and credit classification | campus, category, limit, delivery mode |

Accept PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, screenshots, or official URLs as appropriate. Prefer original downloads and screenshots that show the source, date, and applicable cohort. Do not upload credentials or private account pages to a public repository.
